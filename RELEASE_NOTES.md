### ⚽ FIFA 14 PC Installer v1.0.0

One-click installer and launcher for **FIFA 14 (PS4, CUSA00128, EUR)** on PC, using your own PKG files or extracted game folder.

---

### ✨ What's included
* **FIFA 14 fix build of shadPS4:** pitch textures and per-stadium grass, working replays, correct jersey names, and correct bloom in both day and night/rain matches (real occlusion queries).
* **60 FPS replays and cutscenes** (game patch applied in memory; toggle in the launcher).
* **No PSN account prompt:** the "signed out" system error is accepted automatically in offline mode.
* **Launcher:** fullscreen/windowed, resolution, game language, brightness (gamma) slider, FPS counter, direct launch.

---

### 📥 How to Install
1. Download **`FIFA14.exe`** from the **Assets** section below.
2. Run it and choose the install folder (~10 GB).
3. Select your **base game PKG** and the **01.06 update PKG**, or your already extracted **`CUSA00128`** folder.
4. Click **Start installation** and play with the **FIFA 14** shortcut.

---

### 💡 Notes
* Requires the **EUR** version (CUSA00128). Update **01.06** is strongly recommended (the 60 FPS patch needs it).
* No game files, PS4 firmware or system fonts are included.
* Online modes do not work (EA's servers are closed).
* Bundled shadPS4: `b72489d` + the patches in [`patches/`](https://github.com/danilenko22/FIFA14-PS4-PC-Installer/tree/main/patches).
* **Windows SmartScreen:** the executable is unsigned. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
